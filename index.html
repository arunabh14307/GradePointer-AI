    AI Privacy & Client-Side Data Storage Notice

    Client-Side Storage: All student coursework, CGPA records, calculation history, study plan tasks, and preferences are stored strictly on your device via the browser's standard LocalStorage API. (Note: LocalStorage is standard, unencrypted client-side browser storage scoped to this origin.)

    AI Service Data Flow: When invoking AI features (Study Planner, Chat Assistant), only the specific academic inputs required for that analysis (such as current/target CGPA, course names, or revision preferences) are transmitted to the Flask backend and official Google Gemini API.

    Institutional Boundary: GradePilot AI does not connect to, query, or store official university student registrar credentials, SIS passwords, or private institutional academic databases.
